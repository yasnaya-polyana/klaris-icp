Read this before trusting a score

The first run put all thirteen accounts in Tier D. That was the model working, not failing — the thresholds had been written as if evidence were complete. Measured capture rate per signal family, averaged across the cohort:

Two families are effectively unreachable from public sources today, and together they carry 40 of the 100 trigger points. Deadline pressure needs Notified Body certificate data (EUDAMED); prior regulatory pain needs systematic FDA warning letter, Form 483 and recall mining. Both are automatable and both are worth building next.

Two fixes shipped. Dual threshold sets — one calibrated to what public research actually yields, one strict for when CRM or enrichment data exists. And a coverage figure on every result, so a low score caused by thin research is visibly distinct from a low score caused by a poor account.

The ordering this model produces is trustworthy. The absolute cut-points are not, yet — recalibrate after ~50 accounts, and again once there is closed-won and closed-lost data to fit against.
